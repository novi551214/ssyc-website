About SSYC

Our Story

Who we are, why we started, and who we welcome.

A space we wish existed sooner

We're a group of students and young professionals at Rutgers and nearby colleges who realized we needed a real, regular space to socialize, stay active, and build genuine friendships right here in Somerset.

Inspired by groups like Young Muslim Piscataway, SSYC is an independent, unique community built for anyone in and around Somerset — a place to make real friends, not just acquaintances. You don't need an invitation, and you don't need to be part of any particular group to show up.

We get together regularly, outside Masjid-e-Ali, for pickup sports — football, soccer, basketball, and more — followed by chit-chat and dinner. Each week's exact plan is shared in our WhatsApp group every Friday. No formality, no pressure — just come as you are.
